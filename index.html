FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

B.Voc (Bachelor of Vocation) is a 3-year undergraduate degree focused on skill-based education. Unlike B.Tech or BCA which are more theory-heavy, B.Voc includes 40-60% practical training, industry internships, and follows NSQF levels. It offers multiple exit points with a Diploma after 1 year and Advanced Diploma after 2 years.

Students who have completed their 10+2 (any stream) from a recognized board are eligible for admission to B.Voc programs. There is no entrance exam — admissions are based on merit and a personal interview.

Yes! B.Voc is a UGC-recognized degree equivalent to any other bachelor's degree. Graduates are eligible for all government jobs, higher education (MBA, MCA, M.Tech), and competitive examinations.

Our fee structure is very affordable compared to private engineering colleges. We also offer EMI options, scholarship schemes for meritorious students, and special discounts for early admissions. Contact us at 9166720175 for detailed fee information.

Absolutely. B.Voc graduates can pursue M.Voc, MBA, MCA, M.Tech, or any other master's program. Several of our alumni have successfully completed post-graduation from reputed universities across India.
